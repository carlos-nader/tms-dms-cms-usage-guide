# Contributing to TMS/DMS/CMS Usage Guide

## TL;DR
Spotted something wrong? Have a suggestion? **Just send an email or post on the BMS forum.** That's it.

---

## 🚀 How to Contribute (Easy Way)

**You don't need to know LaTeX, Git, or anything technical.**

The [guide](https://github.com/carlos-nader/tms-dms-cms-usage-guide/blob/main/guide.pdf) is available as a **PDF** — just read or download it and let us know what needs fixing!

### Found an issue or have a suggestion?

📧 **Email:** [carlos.snm@gmail.com](mailto:carlos.snm@gmail.com)  
💬 **Forum:** [Post on Falcon BMS Forum Thread](https://forum.falcon-bms.com/topic/32541/in-development-tms-dms-cms-usage-guide-public-project/15)

**Include:**
- What needs to be changed (which section/table)
- Why (wrong info, unclear, missing something)
- Your suggested fix (if you have one)

---

## 🔧 Advanced Contributions (Optional)

Want to submit or review complete sections, chapters or help with LaTeX/structure or maybe a WIP?

📂 **Check these resources:**
- `/docs/` — Briefing Document, Naming Conventions, Style Rules
- `/templates/` — Base LaTeX template for new content
- `/WIP/` — Current WIP files for your reading, consultation or fixes
- **GitHub Issues** — Track active work and open tasks

**Submit your work:**
- Email `.tex` files to [carlos.snm@gmail.com](mailto:carlos.snm@gmail.com)
- Open a **GitHub Issue/Pull Request** if you're comfortable with Git
- Or look the **Official Guide Post** on [Falcon BMS Forum](https://forum.falcon-bms.com/topic/32541/in-development-tms-dms-cms-usage-guide-public-project/15)
